Hi, I'm **Rupesh** a Data Scientist & Software Engineer interested in deep learning, algorithms, and ideas that bridge theory and practice.

This blog is where I think out loud: working through papers, books, and problems I find interesting. Posts range from technical deep-dives to book notes and half-formed opinions.

## What you'll find here

- **ML & deep learning** — annotated paper reads, from-scratch implementations, architecture walkthroughs
- **Algorithms** — notes from courses like MIT 6.006, with worked examples
- **Books** — notes and reflections on books in rationality, cognitive science, and more
- **Series** — longer explorations split into ordered parts, so you can follow a thread from basics to depth

## How this site works

Posts in the same **series** are linked in order — you'll see a navigation box at the top of each part showing where you are in the thread and what comes next.

Use the **Archive** to browse by date, **Tags** to browse by topic, and the **🔍 search** (or press `/`) to find anything specific.
